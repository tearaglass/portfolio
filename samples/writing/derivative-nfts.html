the cultural lineage of derivative nfts

by teara · MAGDELINO · 2024

as lame as mostly all of it definitely is, nft communities, crypto culture, and whatever people think “ai art” is, are the newest prosumer infestations growing up through the cracks in the tombstones of the pseudo-edgy, postmodern, DIY, internet art graveyard. on a nice, balmy day, it could be regarded as an efficient and collaborative socio-financial experiment, one where it’s easy for any curious person to get lost in the uncertainly lit halls of innovative potential, lonely but not so alone.

derivatives not only help keep the original works relevant, they invite new creators into the fold. the effort and dedication it takes to produce a thoughtful cultural homage fosters and engages a community and encourages it to thrive on its own momentum.

derivative nfts are a very effective and unique way of amplifying the cultural impact and viral potential of their parent collections, often pushing the valuation of the original collection higher, which produces a positive loop of attention and price.

for new collectors, derivatives offer exposure to the vision behind a community ethos (and, investors hope, price action to follow) of the originals, but can “buy in” at a much lower cost.

derivative collections push boundaries in their own way. by remixing the dna of established projects, they bring new styles, ideas, and stories to the table. this keeps the dubiously necessary but certainly entertaining crypto art social space alive, competitive, and dynamic, ensuring that the most iconic collections evolve instead of stagnate.

in fact, a collection that lends itself to evolving, to being endlessly iterated upon, is perhaps the most important characteristic of a good meme.

created by remilia corporation, milady isn’t just a collection of 10,000 anime girl pics; it’s really a whole vibe.

milady is pretty much a love letter to creepycute japanese fashion and youth culture, early 2000s internet nostalgia, and chaotic, ironic “it-girl” energy. there’s also a vague, kinda endearingly delusional obsession with “victory”/ “being victorious”.

whose victory? who knows! maybe yours? victory over what? well, iykyk.

milady fans are rabid devotees, and old heads are able to identify if a derivative is to be disregarded and ignored (a “soulless cash-grab”), or expresses an appropriately reverent love letter to milady, which the artist felt compelled to express in remixing the milady standard aesthetic. these new forms reflect the community’s dynamism, a space where entirely new dialogues begin to emerge and contort.

mfers: meme minimalism

stupid-maxxing via aesthetic distillation

the 'mfers' collection features 10,000 stick-figure characters, each reclining in a chair with headphones, embodying a laid-back, irreverent attitude. derivatives of mfers pull humor from the OG’s spirit of minimalism, stretching the concept in new and odd ways, often proving

that even the simplest and most juvenile looking art can

carry deep cultural resonance.

created by the anonymous artist “sartoshi”, a pseudonym which: “combined (sic.) 'satoshi' (Bitcoin's creator) and 'art'”......le wow;

mfers is simple, irreverent, and wildly relatable. i mean, they’re stick figures, so there’s minimal room for larping. “no promises, no roadmap—just vibes.”

sartoshi handed the project’s leadership over to the mfers community in 2022, a decision which formalized key points of the mfers brand: decentralization, authentically art-driven, playfulness, and a grassroots community.

decentralization, post-authorship, cc0, copy-left, whatever you want to call it, is a larger ideological trend in crypto art which sartoshi certainly carried a banner for and successfully contributed to with mfers. this idealogical trend is also of particular aspergian interest to the remilia corp. godhead ceo, charlotte fang.

both collections, and their enigmatic founders, very publicly welcome anyone who feels inspired by the images to engage with, build on top of, and draw inspiration from the OG idea/brand.

essential mixes: for the culture

the milady mfers collection (floor price: .0039 ETH/~$15) is a perfect cultural cocktail that gets the job done: the subcultural, beatnik, cool kid hacker vibes of milady meets mfers’ meme-fueled, irreverent, infantile, chaotic scribbles.

the resulting imagery is something greater than the sum of its parts. a hilarious fusion of aesthetics and philosophies that perfectly capture the feelings of absurdity, humor, and, in this writer’s opinion, defiant creative potential.

the milady mfers collection is a clever cultural artifact, and a

reminder that the most fantastically bizarre elements of nft culture thrive on remixing, reinterpreting, and constantly trying to figure itself out.

milady mfers / bored miladys

bored milady maker, (floor price: 0.09 ETH ~$334) is another excellent example of peak derivative culture, mashing up the neochibi vibes of milady maker with the hyper-recognizable traits of the puke-tastically popular bored ape yacht club (bayc) nfts. it's a fusion that's barely even homage or an ironic flex. BMILLY carves out a new space for fans of both collections, fans of just one of them, or people who hate both. bored miladys is pure remix, where punk rock flair is strangling the subject into total image irrelevance. it’s less about the original collections, and more about how a visual can be crafted to represent a specific allegory based on real events from community folklore.

why milady mfers is the move

as of this article’s writing, milady maker sits at a floor price of 6.5 ETH ($23,000), with mfers at .36 ETH ($1280), out-of-reach for newer collectors, especially those unfamiliar with this kind of bat-shit digital asset.

milady mfers occupies a rare position wherein cultural cachet meets accessibility for the impoverished among us. with a floor price of 0.0079 eth, it’s an easy entry point for new collectors, who can now become a part of two iconic and proven memetic lineages if they want. or they can just look at their funny little pic. whatever you’re into, man.

either way, it’s a low-risk, high-reward play that’s both financially smart and culturally resonant.

owning a milady mfer isn’t just about owning a part of a few memes—it shows you choose to align with the ethos of remix culture and decentralized creativity. it is your freedom to nonchalantly signal, “yeah, i get it”.

you are prepared for what’s coming, unfazed by a world where social and cultural alliances are shifting fast enough to cause permanent whiplash.

an ultimate commitment to the grind

your personal relationships will remain on hold while you steadily grind your way towards offsetting your uber eats budget.

it’s time to get serious. you must lock-in.

what better way to show your maddening commitment to the hustle, (maybe even your

allegiance to your most precious fiscal ideology...?), than with a right-facing pfp?

milady mfers are instantly relatable imagery, even appearing to stare intently at a screen. just like us. i mean, they’re literally embodying the posture of someone who clearly “never stops clicking”??! hello???!

this design evolution is a far, despairing cry (maybe a cackle? idk) from sartoshi’s “laid-back, irreverent attitude” back in 2022.

milady mfers is the ultimate symbol of dedication to 3 core pillars: Your Grind, Your Focus, and Your Unshakable Belief in Divine Chaos and Fated Opportunities, which this fertile landscape of new capital could provide in spades to those who go digging for it.

milady mfers is more than just a pfp—it’s a statement, a cultural timestamp, and a ticket to a community that’s comfy, if a bit cracked, and making it through the trenches.
