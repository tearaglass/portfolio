Interactive website · 2022 · Omakasea

PokeGAN / Geomint

I built PokeGAN's user-facing website as an explorable RPG Maker MZ world. Visitors met characters, discovered whitelist interactions, and used a location-based mint experience. The exported game was the website, rather than a game embedded inside a separate site.

My contribution

I assembled the maps, customized template-based sprites to resemble our Bali team, wrote character dialogue, and built the game's interaction and event logic. I used provided and third-party map assets. The development team supplied the backend, blockchain and location-verification functionality.

The experience

Before mint day, visitors could talk to characters to join the whitelist. For Geomint, participants visited designated art museums. Location eligibility was handled off-chain. When it was recognized, a character directed them to a patch of grass, where the game presented the mint quantity interaction and handed off to the wallet.

The implementation challenge

The difficult part was connecting game events to wallet and blockchain events. The recovered export contains separate states for eligibility checks, permission, quantity selection, mint requests and success. I designed the dialogue and interaction logic around those states, working with the developers responsible for the external systems.
