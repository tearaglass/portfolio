# Practice Statement

I build systems that behave like small worlds. A project may be a browser
instrument, a game, a live visual environment, a community tool, or a fictional
institution. Across those forms I’m interested in what happens when an idea is
given rules, memory, texture, and a way for someone else to enter it.

My materials are sound, code, images, interfaces, writing, social systems, and
the practical structures that allow creative work to reach people. I often begin
with something funny, excessive, or slightly impossible, then build it seriously
enough that it develops its own behavior.

My background in music, cultural anthropology, and art history informs an
attention to participation, ritual, objects, archives, and collective life. My
recent work brings those questions into creative AI workflows, browser software,
generative visuals, games, and livestream environments.

I want technology to feel less like sterile infrastructure and more like culture:
intimate, playable, strange, and alive.

## Recurring interests

- Sound as interface and environment
- Software as a place people can inhabit
- Cultural systems, archives, and collective behavior
- Practical tools for artists and small creative teams
