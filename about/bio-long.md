# Long Bio

Delaney Mills is an artist, musician, and creative technologist working across
sound, software, games, internet culture, and interactive media. She builds
human-centered systems that make complex ideas tangible: browser instruments,
live visual tools, simulations, games, community infrastructure, and fictional
institutions.

Her recent practice is strongly AI-assisted, but not AI-dependent. She uses
coding agents and generative tools as collaborators inside a disciplined process
of prototyping, testing, documentation, and revision. The result is work that
treats AI as a way to expand creative and technical capacity while keeping
authorship, taste, safety, and human judgment visible.

Mills’ background in music, cultural anthropology, and art history gives her a
particular interest in how people form cultures around objects, technologies,
symbols, and one another. She has also worked in touring and production, gallery
management, political field organizing, community operations, startup management,
and client coordination.

She is a useful consultant for teams working between technology and culture:
someone who can generate strong concepts, understand users and communities,
coordinate messy projects, write clearly, and turn a speculative direction into
a working artifact.
