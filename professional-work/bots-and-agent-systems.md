# Bots and agent systems

## What it is

An independent body of work using bots and AI-assisted workflows for community memory, creative coding, planning, and repetitive coordination.

## What Delaney made

- A local launcher and management system for separate Three.js, Strudel, and OBS Assets specialist bots.
- Shared start, status, stop, logging, and health-check workflows while keeping each specialist focused on its own domain.
- Discord and Telegram systems, including community tooling and structured agent workflows.

## Why it matters

This work shows practical applied AI judgment: separating responsibilities, making state visible, and keeping a human responsible for tone, trust, and decisions.

## Status

Multiple prototypes and active local systems. Public examples should use demo data or redacted screenshots.
