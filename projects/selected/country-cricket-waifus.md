## Short description

Country Cricket Waifus is a text-light browser cricket game built around timing,
shot choice, bowling decisions, and a small visual field. It uses an intentionally
compact match format to make a strange sports-world prototype playable quickly.

## What I explored

I explored how to make a cricket game readable through motion, icons, timing
windows, and character presence rather than a wall of written instructions.

## What I made

The prototype supports two innings, batting and bowling roles, timing-based
shots, directional contact, ball flight, moving fielders, catches, boundaries,
wickets, strike rotation, and repeated super overs for ties. The controls are
keyboard- and touch-friendly, with the current role shown through the central
bat/ball indicator.
