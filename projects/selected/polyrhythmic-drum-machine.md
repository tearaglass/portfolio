## Short description

Polyrhythmic Drum Machine is a browser instrument for working with linked
clocks, swing, synthesized percussion, MIDI, and recording. Its central idea is
to make timing relationships visible and playable instead of hiding them behind
a conventional step sequencer.

## What I explored

I explored how independent clocks can feel physical and musical when their phase,
ratio, drift, and relationship are exposed as performance controls.

## What I made

The instrument includes linked clock modes, a synthesized kick, drumhead-impact
voice, two-click monitoring, swing, MIDI output, orbital visualization, and a
browser recording path. Audio, MIDI, visualization, and configuration are kept
as separate modules so the instrument can be modified without losing its feel.
